The platform

Two modules. One governance backbone.

OpticVector AI builds for the government that consumes AI — not the labs that build it. Each module ships compliance-native and is designed to reinforce the other.

● Live in production

TRAIGA Auditor

AI-governance compliance (GRC)

Discover the AI your government already runs, judge it against Texas HB 149 (TRAIGA), and defend the city with regulator-ready artifacts — all from live, re-verifiable evidence.

  • Four discovery channels — outside-in scanner, staff-usage DLP, procurement import, and council-agenda mining — resolving to one canonical AI registry.
  • Compliance engine validates disclosure, timing, dark-pattern and privacy rules; every failure opens a 60-day cure clock.
  • Generates the artifacts a regulator asks for: AG Response Package, Written Statement of Cure, and a Safe-Harbor alignment to a 14-control NIST AI RMF profile.
  • Fail-secure by design — a blocked or failed scan is never reported clean.
● In development

GovAssist AI

Grounded citizen concierge · optional avatar

A white-labeled municipal concierge that answers only from a city's own published information — bilingual by design, accessible by default, with an optional digital-human avatar layer.

  • Strict retrieval-grounding: cite the source, or defer to the right department. Never bluffs a civic answer.
  • Independent English & Spanish paths — not round-tripped machine translation of statutory text.
  • Accessible-first: WCAG 2.2 AA text/voice widget is the default; the avatar is an optional premium layer, never the sole channel.
  • Zero-PII, one-line embed as a Web Component — drops into any CMS with no style collisions.
